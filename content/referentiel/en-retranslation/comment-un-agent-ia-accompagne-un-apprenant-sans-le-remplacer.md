> An AI learning support agent is a conversational system, available at all times and without judgement, that answers the learner's questions, rephrases explanations and gives immediate feedback. It does not replace the trainer: it provides a teaching presence between the moments of human intervention and frees the trainer from repetitive questions. Its effectiveness depends on the quality of the corpus on which it is configured and on regular quality control by subject-matter experts.

### What the agent does well

Answering comprehension questions in real time, without waiting for the next session with the trainer. The wait between a question and its answer can last hours or days. This wait interrupts the momentum of learning and can lead to dropping out.

Rephrasing an explanation in several ways until the learner understands. This ability to rephrase on demand is particularly useful for learners who understand things in different ways.

Giving immediate feedback on an answer or a piece of work. This kind of feedback helps the learner correct their understanding without waiting for the next formal assessment.

Maintaining engagement at moments of discouragement. An agent can detect signs of difficulty and suggest a break, a change of approach or encouragement suited to the context.

### What the agent does not do

It does not make a clinical diagnosis of deep-seated learning difficulties. It does not handle complex emotional situations. It does not assess interpersonal or behavioural skills. It does not replace the human relationship that motivates persistence in the effort.

### The conditions for a reliable agent

A learning agent is only reliable if it is configured on a corpus validated by subject-matter experts. An agent that answers from a general-purpose language model without specific configuration will produce answers that are fluent, confident and sometimes factually incorrect.

The minimum configuration requires three elements: a validated document base (training content, skills frameworks, relevant regulatory texts), rules of use (what the agent may answer and what it must escalate to a trainer), and regular quality control by experts who check the relevance and accuracy of the answers produced.

### The right operational balance

The most effective deployments separate the roles clearly. The agent handles volume: repetitive comprehension questions, requests for examples, reminders of procedures. The trainer handles depth: support with complex learning, emotionally charged situations, and the assessment of skills that require human judgement.

### Frequently asked questions

**Can the agent give wrong information?**
Yes, like any AI system. This risk is reduced by rigorous configuration on a validated corpus and by regular quality control. It is not eliminated.

**Do learners prefer the agent or the trainer?**
Both, for different reasons. The agent is preferred for repetitive questions and revision. The trainer is preferred for complex learning and the relationship.

**Does the agent learn from its interactions?**
It depends on the system. This feature must be documented in the GDPR processing agreement and disclosed to learners in the privacy notice.
