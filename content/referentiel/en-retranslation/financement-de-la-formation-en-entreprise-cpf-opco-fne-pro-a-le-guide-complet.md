> In France, companies can fund their employees' training through several schemes: the OPCO (skills operators), which cover part of the costs for SMEs; the CPF (personal training account), to which the employer can make top-up contributions; FNE-Formation, which can be used in the event of cyclical difficulties; and Pro-A (retraining or promotion through work-study), for employees who are changing career. Each scheme has its own eligibility conditions, ceilings and contacts.

### The OPCO: standard funding

The skills operators (OPCO) are the bodies that collect companies' training contributions. Since the 2018 reform, they have replaced the OPCA. There are eleven of them, each covering one or more business sectors depending on the collective agreement applicable to the company.

For companies with fewer than 50 employees, the OPCO can cover all or part of the tuition costs of training in the PDC, up to hourly ceilings set by each OPCO. These ceilings vary according to the scheme, the training activity and the sector. For companies with 50 or more employees, OPCO funding is mainly reserved for specific schemes (work-study, Pro-A, CPF de transition).

The procedure consists of submitting a funding application before the training begins. An application submitted after the activity has started is systematically refused by most OPCO.

### The CPF topped up by the employer

The personal training account (CPF) is credited automatically every year for every employee: 500 euros per year for a full-time employee, up to a limit of 5,000 euros. This ceiling rises to 800 euros per year and 8,000 euros in total for employees without qualifications.

The employer can top up an employee's CPF, that is, pay additional funds into it to finance training whose cost exceeds the available balance. This top-up can be provided for by a company or sector agreement, or offered unilaterally by the employer. It is not subject to social security contributions up to certain ceilings.

The strategic benefit of a CPF top-up for the employer is twofold. It makes it possible to fund expensive training without charging the full expense to the PDC budget, and it acts as a retention lever.

### FNE-Formation

The Fonds national de l'emploi-Formation (FNE-Formation) is a scheme that can be used in periods of economic difficulty. It allows companies on short-time working or undergoing restructuring to fund training for their employees, with enhanced cover from the State.

The eligibility conditions have changed since the health crisis. In 2026, FNE-Formation is refocused on companies facing major economic change: digital transition, energy transition, reorganisation linked to the introduction of AI into processes. It can be used in conjunction with the relevant DREETS (Direction régionale de l'économie, de l'emploi, du travail et des solidarités).

### Pro-A: retraining or promotion through work-study

Pro-A (reconversion ou promotion par alternance) allows an employee to change occupation or obtain career advancement through a scheme combining training and work, on the work-study model.

It is open to employees whose qualification level is below a bachelor's degree (licence, bac +3) and who have not obtained a diploma for more than a year. The training is delivered by an accredited provider and leads to a recognised qualification.

The sector OPCO covers the tuition costs up to an hourly ceiling defined by sector agreement. The employee keeps their pay during the training.

### The training plan funded by the statutory contribution

Every company pays a statutory contribution to vocational training, calculated on its payroll. For companies with fewer than 11 employees, the rate is 0.55% of gross payroll. For companies with 11 or more employees, it is 1%. This contribution is collected by the sector OPCO and can partly fund the training activities in the PDC.

### Comparison table of the schemes

| Scheme | Beneficiary | Main condition | Funding |
|---|---|---|---|
| OPCO (training plan) | Company | Fewer than 50 employees prioritised | Partial or full cover depending on OPCO ceiling |
| Topped-up CPF | Employee + employer | Prior agreement, insufficient CPF balance | Direct payment into the CPF account |
| FNE-Formation | Company | Economic change, short-time working | Enhanced State cover |
| Pro-A | Employee | Qualification level < bac+3 | OPCO cover according to sector agreement |
| CPF de transition | Employee | Career change, approved project | Funding by CPIR (Commissions paritaires interprofessionnelles régionales) |
