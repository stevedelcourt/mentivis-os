> Since Article 4 of the AI Act came into application, every organisation that uses AI systems must ensure that its teams are proficient in them, and be able to demonstrate it. The question is no longer whether to train, but how to prove that the training has produced real proficiency, skill by skill, and how to fund it. This pillar brings together the regulatory reference points, the method of proof and the funding schemes.

### The regulatory framework

The obligation has applied since February 2025 and covers deployers, that is, any organisation using AI under its own authority. The text was clarified in 2026, but the obligation to act remains. Understanding what AI can and cannot do in training helps to calibrate a realistic programme, neither undersized nor based on promises. See [What AI cannot do in training](/referentiel/ce-que-lia-ne-peut-pas-faire-en-formation).

### Proof, not just training

Completing a module does not prove proficiency. The expected compliance rests on a real measurement of skill acquisition, documented and usable by an auditor. The method is set out in [Mandatory AI training: how to prove compliance with Article 4](/referentiel/formation-ia-obligatoire-comment-prouver-la-conformite-a-larticle-4-de-lai-act), and the measurement principle in [What real skills measurement is and why it changes everything for HR management](/referentiel/quest-ce-que-la-mesure-reelle-des-competences-et-pourquoi-elle-change-tout).

### Funding

A compliance obligation can be partly addressed through existing funding schemes, subject to conditions. See [Funding AI training for your teams](/referentiel/financer-la-formation-ia-de-vos-equipes-opco-fne-cpf-ai-act).

### Frequently asked questions

**Who is covered by the AI training obligation?**
Any organisation that uses an AI system in its activity, including through consumer tools, falls within the scope of the deployers covered by Article 4.

**Is proof of attendance enough?**
No. The expected compliance concerns demonstrable proficiency, measured skill by skill, not exposure to content.

**How does MentivisOS help with compliance?**
By producing traceability of skill acquisition skill by skill, directly usable for a compliance file and for a funder.
