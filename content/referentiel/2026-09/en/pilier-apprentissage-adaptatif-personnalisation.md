> Adaptive learning adjusts the content, difficulty and sequence of a learning path to the learner's actual prior learning. Its cognitive foundations (retrieval practice, spaced repetition, difficulty calibration) are among the best established. But effectiveness depends on the implementation, not the label. This pillar brings together how it works, what personalisation changes for the learner, and how to distinguish a genuine system from a facade.

### How adaptation works

Adaptation applies to the content itself: what is presented, in what order, at what level, with what assessment. How it works is described in [What is adaptive learning and why it works](/referentiel/quest-ce-que-lapprentissage-adaptatif-et-pourquoi-ca-fonctionne) and [How AI adapts learning to each individual](/referentiel/comment-lia-adapte-lapprentissage-a-chaque-individu).

### What personalisation changes

Training time falls because learners do not go back over what they have already mastered, and engagement rises because the content is calibrated to their actual level. See [How AI personalises and adapts a learning path for each individual](/referentiel/comment-lia-personnalise-et-adapte-un-parcours-de-formation).

### Distinguishing the mechanism from the facade

A genuine system recalculates the learning path at each step and measures acquisition; a facade only adjusts the pace. Evidence is separated from marketing in [Adaptive learning in the workplace: what really works](/referentiel/adaptive-learning-en-entreprise-ce-qui-marche-vraiment), with further light shed by [Learning with AI: what the research really says](/referentiel/apprendre-avec-lia-ce-que-les-etudes-disent-vraiment).

### Frequently asked questions

**Is adaptive learning proven?**
Its cognitive mechanisms are among the best established. Evidence on specific implementations varies from system to system.

**Does it work for every subject?**
It is more effective for structured, assessable skills, and less so for those that require human judgement of quality.

**How can you recognise a genuine adaptive system?**
By checking that personalisation is recalculated at each step, that acquisition is measured and spread over time, and that the system relies on a structured skills framework.
