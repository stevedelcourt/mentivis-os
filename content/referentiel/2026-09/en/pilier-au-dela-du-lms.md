> An LMS solves the distribution of training. It does not solve personalisation at scale, the measurement of acquisition or the updating of content. These gaps are not configuration faults but limits of the category. This pillar distinguishes the families of tools, explains why training is no longer enough without generation and measurement, and sets out the questions to ask in order to choose without mistaking one category for another.

### Distinguishing the families of tools

LMS, LXP, adaptive learning and AI training engine partly overlap and are often confused. The distinction is made in [LMS, LXP, adaptive learning, AI training engine: how to tell them apart](/referentiel/lms-lxp-adaptive-learning-moteur-de-formation-ia-comment-les-distinguer).

### Why distribution is no longer enough

A module produced once and distributed to everyone ignores each person's actual prior learning, and completion does not prove acquisition. The reasoning is developed in [The LMS is no longer enough](/referentiel/le-lms-ne-suffit-plus-ce-quun-systeme-de-formation-ia-fait-de-plus) and [Why corporate training is no longer enough without AI](/referentiel/pourquoi-la-formation-en-entreprise-ne-suffit-plus-sans-ia).

### Choosing without mistaking the category

The LMS market is crowded. Positioning an offering against an AI training engine, then asking the right questions, avoids buying one category in place of another. See [Alternatives to LMS for training with AI](/referentiel/alternative-aux-lms-pour-former-avec-lia-panorama) and [How to choose an AI training system: the right questions to ask](/referentiel/comment-choisir-un-systeme-de-formation-ia-les-bonnes-questions-a-poser).

### Frequently asked questions

**Should you replace your LMS?**
Not necessarily. An AI training engine can complement an existing LMS via API or replace it, depending on the infrastructure.

**Is an LMS with AI equivalent to an AI training engine?**
No, if the AI is limited to recommending or creating content. The decisive capability is generating learning paths and measuring acquisition.

**Does the LMS become useless?**
No. It remains useful for distribution and access. It simply does not cover acquisition, dynamic personalisation and updating.
