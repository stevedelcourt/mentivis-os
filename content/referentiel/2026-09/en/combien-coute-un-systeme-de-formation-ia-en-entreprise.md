> The cost of an AI training system is not read from a price list but from a scope. It depends on the number of learners, the frameworks to be integrated, the level of integration with existing tools and the features activated. This is why serious providers offer pricing on request rather than a published price: a single price for different scopes would be misleading. The useful question is not "what is the price" but "what return on what scope", because a well-targeted system reduces costs that the training budget never consolidates.

### What makes up the cost

Four items make up the real cost, beyond the licence. The platform itself, over the term of the commitment. Integration with existing tools, notably the HRIS and the LMS. Configuration and deployment, including the integration of your skills frameworks. Training for the teams who will run the system. These items should be requested explicitly from the provider, with contractual commitments, not indicative estimates.

### Why comparing against the platform budget alone is a mistake

Comparing the cost of an AI training system with the budget of an existing platform alone distorts the analysis. The right scope includes the hidden costs that traditional training bears without measuring them: the time employees spend on content they have already mastered, the repetition of training on subjects poorly acquired the first time, undetected gaps, and the cost of compliance that is not secured. These costs are real but rarely consolidated.

### What the system reduces

A system that personalises mechanically reduces the time spent on content that is already known. It reduces the need to repeat training. It produces traceability that secures funding. It makes progress measurable, which allows training to be linked to an operational effect. Documented cases in the sector put training time savings in the order of twenty to forty per cent on comparable scopes, which remains to be verified on your own scope.

### How to obtain a reliable estimate

Isolate a limited scope: a team, a job role, a priority skill. Measure the current training costs on that scope. Project the time savings and the secured funding gains. Compare with the cost of the system over the same period. This approach produces a credible estimate, specific to your organisation, far more useful than a catalogue price. ### Frequently asked questions

**Why is the price not published?**
Because it depends on the scope. A single price applied to different sizes and needs would be misleading.

**Does an AI training system cost more than an LMS?**
The licence may be comparable or higher, but the scope of the assessment is not the same. An LMS delivers, an AI training system generates and measures, and reduces costs that an LMS does not touch.

**How long before the return is measurable?**
On a well-targeted deployment, the first measurable effects are generally observed between six and eighteen months, depending on the maturity of the organisation.

**Can the cost be funded?**
The associated training may qualify for vocational training funding schemes, subject to conditions. See [Funding your teams' AI training](/referentiel/financer-la-formation-ia-de-vos-equipes-opco-fne-cpf-ai-act).
